Greenside Founding Club: an app in your club's own name, logo and colours in the App Store and Google Play, plus web software for the club to run members, tee times, competitions, invoices and the bar tab. Members log in with a code by e-mail, book tee times with guests and extras, pay invoices with iDEAL and show a club pass QR code at the driving range. Handicaps and scores stay in GOLF.NL, with a button to it in the app; for direct debit we deliver a SEPA collection file that you upload to your own bank.
No double work and no double bill: during a three-month pilot your volunteers keep working in your current system only; every week we copy your member list from it into Greenside ourselves. After the pilot you pay nothing until the day you cancel your old system, and the first invoice falls in your next budget year. Before the pilot we agree in writing what counts as success (for example: 40% of members active in the app); if we don't reach it, or you simply want to stop, you pay nothing.
If the two founders stop, your club is safe, and it says so in the contract: every month a full export of your members, invoices and bookings goes automatically to your club's own storage; the source code is held in escrow and released to the clubs; an IT partner keeps the platform running for 12 months; and you get 12 months' notice.
We do the work: import, invitations, a printed guide and two Saturday app clinics at the clubhouse for older members, a helpline in the first four weeks, and anything a member can do in the app the desk can also do for them. For your members' meeting you get a ready-made proposal, a short presentation, a cost comparison and answers to the usual questions, and one of us comes along if you like.
Price: € 0.65 per member per month (€ 7.80 per member per year, minimum € 249 a month), about € 618 a month for an 18-hole club with 950 members. Founding clubs (the first ten, pilot starting before 1 July 2027) pay € 0.49 per member (€ 5.88 per member per year), fixed for two years, no migration fee. No commission on what members buy.
Two founders build and support it, with their names and phone numbers on everything. Golfclub Zwolle starts first; once it is live you can see its results page and call its board before you decide.
