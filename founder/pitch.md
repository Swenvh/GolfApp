Greenside: an app in your club's own name, logo and colours in the App Store and Google Play, plus web software for the club to run members, tee times, competitions, invoices and the bar tab.
Members log in with a code by e-mail (no passwords), book tee times with guests and extras, see their invoices and pay with iDEAL, and show a club pass QR code at the driving range. Volunteers get one "mission control" page that says in plain language what needs doing today.
We import your members from E-Golf4U, Nexxchange, IntoGolf or Excel and invite them all to the app in one go. No commission on what members buy: the money is the club's.
Price per month: € 249 (9 holes), € 449 (18 holes), € 599 (27+ holes), plus a one-off migration fee of € 750 to € 1,500. Founding clubs (the first ten): € 399 a month, fixed for two years.
You start with a three-month pilot, then a yearly contract. Two founders build and support it; the first pilot club is Golfclub Zwolle (not live yet).
