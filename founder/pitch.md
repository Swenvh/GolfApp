Greenside Founding Club: an app in your club's own name, logo and colours in the App Store and Google Play, plus web software for the club to run members, tee times, competitions, invoices and the bar tab. Members log in with a code by e-mail, book tee times with guests and extras, pay invoices with iDEAL and show a club pass QR code at the driving range.
No risk to try: you start with a three-month pilot for your board and a group of members while your current system keeps running. Before it starts we agree in writing what counts as success (for example: 40% of members active in the app). If we don't reach it, or you simply want to stop, you stop after the pilot and pay nothing: no licence, no migration fee.
Your data stays yours: you can export all members, invoices and bookings at any time as a spreadsheet file. The contract says that if Greenside ever stops, you get 12 months' notice and a full handover of your data.
We do the work: we import your members from E-Golf4U, Nexxchange, IntoGolf or Excel, invite them, give you a printed guide for older members and run a helpline in the first four weeks, Saturday mornings included.
Price per month after the pilot: € 249 (9 holes), € 449 (18 holes), € 599 (27+ holes). Founding clubs (the first ten) pay € 179 (9 holes) or € 399 (18+ holes), fixed for two years, and no migration fee. No commission on what members buy.
Two founders build and support it. Golfclub Zwolle starts first; once it is live you can call its board before you decide.
