# CFO: Greenside

**Read this first.** The tool prints `€ ` and "a day"; here every amount is in **euros, excl.
21% VAT**, and "at once" means **clubs paying at the same time**. One unit = one paying club for
one month. Inputs: `founder/numbers.json` (founders unpaid, today's reality) and
`founder/numbers-met-salaris.json` (same, plus € 2,500 a month for each founder, the founders'
target). Prices from `founder/pricing.md`. Sources and estimates: `founder/cfo-sources.md`.
Not financial, tax or legal advice: have an accountant check the structure, payroll costs and
VAT before money moves.

## The CFO's note

**The margin.** Each paying club leaves **€ 290 a month** (80%) at the blended founding price
of € 364 (€ 179 / € 399 / € 499 by course size), after onboarding, the free pilot and the
walk-away guarantee (€ 55), hosting (€ 10) and the club's Apple account (€ 8.25). Running costs
without pay are only **€ 373 a month**, so **2 paying clubs cover them**. The software is cheap to
run; that is not the problem.

**The line to watch: founder pay.** € 2,500 each makes fixed costs € 5,373 a month. Break-even
becomes **19 clubs at the founding price, 16 at list price**. Only ten clubs get the founding
price, so the real number is **10 founding + 8 list = 18 clubs** (7% of the 263 NGF clubs, and
within the ~25 two people can serve). Every other what-if moves year 1 by a few hundred euros; pay
moves it by € 60,000.

**The ramp is the second line.** Year 1 assumes Zwolle runs a free pilot in months 1–3 and pays
from month 4, and that the next clubs only sign after Zwolle has proof (month 11: 2, month 12:
3). The panel backs this: 0 of 20 buy now and 19 of 20 wait for Zwolle. At that ramp year 1
brings in **€ 4,368**. Even a faster ramp (5 clubs by month 12) only gives € 7,280.

**Cash.**
- Founders unpaid: you need **€ 9,621** before it pays for itself, mostly the start-up spend of
  € 7,923 (lawyer € 2,500, security test € 5,000). Year 1 operating result: **€ -993**.
- Founders paid € 2,500 each from month 1: you need **€ 68,916** in year 1, and at 3 clubs you still
  lose about € 4,500 a month.
- The middle way: **pay grows with the clubs.** Everything above € 373 a month goes to the founders.
  At 3 clubs that is about € 500 a month for the two of you; the full € 2,500 each comes at ~18
  clubs. On this ramp that is year 2 to 3, and it needs savings, part-time work or outside money
  to bridge.

**Three ways to improve the margin** (what-ifs; unpaid / paid € 2,500 each):
1. **Fewer free pilots that walk away** (1 in 10 instead of 1 in 3; onboarding € 55 → € 37):
   year 1 € -993 → € -777; break-even with pay 19 → **18 clubs**. Written success criteria
   (bonus B) are what lower this.
2. **Expo Starter (USD 19) instead of Production (USD 99) while there are few apps**: fixed costs
   –€ 72; year 1 € -993 → **€ -129**.
3. **Zwolle pays from month 1** instead of after a free pilot (it is the reference club, not a
   prospect): year 1 € -993 → **€ -122**.
Price itself was set by `/founder-pricing` (9 holes € 249 → € 199, 27+ founding € 399 → € 499,
no "two months free"); together that raised the founding mix from € 344 to € 364.

**The board's money conditions** (`founder/board.md`):
- *Cost to win one club, cost to run one club, clubs to break even:* **answered, as estimates.**
  Winning a club costs about € 850 cash (guide, travel, three months' free running) plus ~40 founder
  hours; with 1 in 3 pilots walking away that is ~€ 1,280 per paying club. Running one costs
  ~€ 19 a month in cash. Break-even: 2 clubs unpaid, 18 with € 2,500 each.
- *A guarantee the business can afford:* **met for the walk-away guarantee** (priced into the
  € 55). The revenue guarantee (L in `founder/offer.md`) stays off: no data on claim rates.
- *At least 3 founding clubs signed before building further:* **not met**; on this ramp that is
  month 12 at the earliest.

**Verdict.** Every club is profitable and running costs are tiny, but **€ 2,500 each needs ~18
clubs**, and clubs wait for Zwolle first. Plan for 1.5 to 3 years before full pay, and decide now
how to bridge it (savings, part-time, a loan or outside money after Zwolle's season).
