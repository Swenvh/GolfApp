# CFO: Greenside

**Read this first.** The tool prints `€ ` and "a day"; here every amount is in **euros, excl.
21% VAT**, and "at once" means **clubs paying at the same time**. One unit = one paying club for
one month. Inputs: `founder/numbers.json` (founders unpaid, today's reality) and
`founder/numbers-met-salaris.json` (same, plus € 2,500 a month for each founder, the founders'
target). Prices from `founder/pricing.md`; costs checked by `/founder-ops` (`founder/ops.md`).
Sources and estimates: `founder/cfo-sources.md`. Not financial, tax or legal advice: have an
accountant check the structure, payroll costs and VAT before money moves.

## The CFO's note

**The margin.** Each paying club leaves **€ 318.50 a month** (88%) at the blended founding price
of € 364 (€ 179 / € 399 / € 499 by course size), after onboarding, the free pilot and the
walk-away guarantee (€ 35) and hosting (€ 10). The club pays its own Apple developer account
(Apple requires the app under the club's name). Running costs without pay are **€ 398 a month**,
so **2 paying clubs cover them**. The software is cheap to run; that is not the problem.

**The line to watch: founder pay.** € 2,500 each makes fixed costs € 5,398 a month. Break-even
becomes **17 clubs at the founding price, 15 at list price**. Only ten clubs get the founding
price, so the real number is **10 founding + 6 list = 16 clubs** (6% of the 263 NGF clubs, and
within the ~25 two people can serve). Every other what-if moves year 1 by a few hundred euros; pay
moves it by € 60,000.

**The ramp is the second line.** Year 1 assumes Zwolle runs a free pilot in months 1–3 and pays
from month 4, and that the next clubs only sign after Zwolle has proof (month 11: 2, month 12:
3). The panel backs this: 0 of 20 buy now and 19 of 20 wait for Zwolle. At that ramp year 1
brings in **€ 4,368**. Even a faster ramp (5 clubs by month 12) only gives € 7,280.

**Cash.**
- Founders unpaid: you need **€ 12,174** before it pays for itself, mostly the start-up spend of
  € 10,423 (security test € 7,500, lawyer € 2,500). Year 1 operating result: **€ -954**.
- Founders paid € 2,500 each from month 1: you need **€ 71,377** in year 1, and at 3 clubs you still
  lose about € 4,400 a month.
- The middle way: **pay grows with the clubs.** Everything above € 398 a month goes to the founders.
  At 3 clubs that is about € 560 a month for the two of you; the full € 2,500 each comes at ~16
  clubs. On this ramp that is year 2 to 3, and it needs savings, part-time work or outside money
  to bridge.

**Three ways to improve the margin** (what-ifs; unpaid / paid € 2,500 each):
1. **Zwolle pays from month 1** instead of after a free pilot (it is the reference club, not a
   prospect): year 1 € -954 → **€ +2**.
2. **Expo Starter (USD 19) instead of Production (USD 99) while there are few apps**: fixed costs
   –€ 72; year 1 € -954 → **€ -90**.
3. **List price for every club after the first ten** (€ 416.50 blended): break-even with pay
   17 → **15 clubs**; year 1 € -954 → € -324 if it applied from the start.
Fewer free pilots that walk away (1 in 10 instead of 1 in 3) matters less now that onboarding is
cheaper: year 1 € -954 → € -846.

**The board's money conditions** (`founder/board.md`):
- *Cost to win one club, cost to run one club, clubs to break even:* **answered, as estimates.**
  Winning a club costs about € 560 cash (printed guide ~€ 300, travel ~€ 200, three months' free
  running) plus ~40 founder hours; with 1 in 3 pilots walking away that is ~€ 830 per paying club.
  Running one costs ~€ 11 a month in cash. Break-even: 2 clubs unpaid, 16 with € 2,500 each.
- *A guarantee the business can afford:* **met for the walk-away guarantee** (priced into the
  € 35). The revenue guarantee (L in `founder/offer.md`) stays off: no data on claim rates.
- *At least 3 founding clubs signed before building further:* **not met**; on this ramp that is
  month 12 at the earliest.

**Verdict.** Every club is profitable and running costs are tiny, but **€ 2,500 each needs ~16
clubs**, and clubs wait for Zwolle first. Plan for 1.5 to 3 years before full pay, and decide now
how to bridge it (savings, part-time, a loan or outside money after Zwolle's season).
