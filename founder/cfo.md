# CFO: Greenside

**Read this first.** The tool prints `€ ` and "a day"; here every amount is in **euros, excl.
21% VAT**, and "a day" means **clubs paying at the same time**. One unit = one paying club for
one month. Inputs: `founder/numbers.json` (founders unpaid, today's reality) and
`founder/numbers-met-salaris.json` (same, plus founder pay). Sources and estimates:
`founder/cfo-sources.md`. Not financial, tax or legal advice: have an accountant check the
structure, payroll costs and VAT before money moves.

## The CFO's note

**The margin.** Each paying club leaves **€ 270 a month** (79%) at the blended founding price
of € 344 (25% at € 179, 75% at € 399), after onboarding, the free pilot and the walk-away
guarantee (€ 55), hosting (€ 10) and the club's Apple account (€ 8.25). Running costs without
pay are only **€ 373 a month**, so **2 paying clubs cover them**. At 10 founding clubs the
margin is 68%. The software is cheap to run; that is not the problem.

**The line to watch: founder pay.** With two founders paid € 4,000 a month each (an estimate;
only you can set it), fixed costs become € 8,373 and break-even jumps to **31 clubs at the
founding price** (24 at list price, € 429 blended). That is more than the 25 clubs two people
can onboard and support (red flag) and 9–12% of the 263 NGF clubs. Every other what-if moves
year 1 by a few hundred euros; pay moves it by € 96,000.

**The ramp is the second line.** Year 1 assumes Zwolle runs a free pilot in months 1–3 and pays
from month 4, and that the next clubs only sign after Zwolle has proof (month 11: 2, month 12:
3). The panel backs this: 0 of 20 buy now and 19 of 20 wait for Zwolle. At that ramp year 1
brings in **€ 4,128**.

**Cash.**
- Without pay: you need **€ 9,761** before it pays for itself, mostly the start-up spend of
  € 7,923 (lawyer € 2,500, security test € 5,000). Year 1 operating result: **€ -1,233**.
- With pay: you need **€ 105,156** in year 1, and it does not stop there: at 3 clubs you still
  lose € 7,562 a month.

**Three ways to improve the margin** (run as what-ifs, founders unpaid / paid):
1. **List price instead of founding price** (€ 429 blended): contribution +€ 85 per club;
   year 1 € -1,233 → **€ -213**; break-even with pay 31 → **24 clubs**. Keep the founding price
   to the first ten clubs, as the pitch says.
2. **Fewer free pilots that walk away** (1 in 10 instead of 1 in 3; onboarding € 55 → € 37):
   contribution € 270 → € 288; break-even with pay 31 → 30; year 1 € -1,017. Agreeing success
   criteria up front (bonus B) is what lowers this.
3. **Expo Starter (USD 19) instead of Production (USD 99) while there are few apps**: fixed costs
   –€ 72; year 1 € -1,233 → **€ -369**. Smaller: the club enrolling its own Apple account saves
   € 8.25 a club-month (year 1 € -1,134), and Zwolle paying from month 1 instead of after a free
   pilot gives € -422.

**The board's money conditions** (`founder/board.md`):
- *Cost to win one club, cost to run one club, clubs to break even:* **answered, as estimates.**
  Winning a club costs about € 850 cash (guide, travel, 3 months' free running) plus ~40 founder
  hours; with 1 in 3 pilots walking away that is ~€ 1,270 per paying club. Running one costs
  ~€ 19 a month in cash. Break-even: 2 clubs without pay, 31 with pay.
- *A guarantee the business can afford:* **met for the walk-away guarantee** (it is priced into
  the € 55). The revenue guarantee (L in `founder/offer.md`) stays off: no data on claim rates.
- *At least 3 founding clubs signed before building further:* **not met**; on this ramp that is
  month 12 at the earliest.

**Verdict.** Greenside is cheap to run and every club is profitable, but **it cannot pay two
salaries for years**: that needs ~30 clubs, and the panel says clubs wait for Zwolle first. Decide
how long you can work unpaid (or part-time), and treat outside money as a question for after
Zwolle's season, when there is proof to show.
