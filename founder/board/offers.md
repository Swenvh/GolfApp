# Offers lens
Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **Buyers are easy to find.** 263 NGF clubs, 275 courses, each with a board you can name. No ad budget is needed to reach the whole market.
- **The dream outcome is real and in money.** Membership fees are about 57% of club income, and members per club are falling. "Keep members and earn more per member and per guest" is a pain a board feels in its budget.
- **The price is small next to the claimed value.** € 449/month (about € 5,400/year) against a founders' estimate of € 19,000–45,000/year. If even the low end were proven, the price could go up.
- **Parts of the stack already cut the buyer's effort:** CSV import from the three incumbents, inviting all members in one go, "mission control" in plain language for volunteers, a pilot before the contract, and a founding-club price for ten clubs that is real scarcity.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Likelihood is close to zero today.** No club has paid, the pilot is not live, and the € 19–45k value is an unmeasured estimate. One of its drivers (pause instead of cancel) has already been removed. A volunteer board buys proof, not estimates.
2. **Effort and risk of switching.** The buyer has to leave E-Golf4U, Nexxchange or IntoGolf, move finance and member data, retrain volunteers and get members to install an app *next to* the free GOLF.NL (325,300 users). If members don't adopt the app, the extras, guest fees and sponsor revenue never show up.
3. **A small, flat ceiling that drifts toward commodity.** The number of clubs is fixed. If every one of the 263 clubs paid the standard tier, that would be only about € 1.4M/year. The headline difference ("no commission") is a price argument, and an incumbent that already sends the invoices can match it.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Which single outcome will you prove at Golfclub Zwolle, how will you measure it, and by when (euros, or members kept)?
- What share of Zwolle's members must actively use the app for the value case to hold, given GOLF.NL is free?
- What do clubs pay their current system today, and what does switching cost them? (/founder-competitors)
- Will a board pay € 449, or more, once the value is proven? (/founder-consumer, then founder-pricing)
- What does it cost you to win and run one club, and how many clubs before you break even? (/founder-cfo)
- What guarantee can you afford? For example: if the app does not earn back its licence in year one, the club gets the difference back.

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Zwolle goes live and, within the three-month pilot, shows measured numbers: member adoption, extras and guest green fees through the app, and hours saved at the desk. These replace the estimate.
2. A written guarantee tied to the "licence earned back" statistic the product already tracks, priced by /founder-cfo.
3. A stack built around the board's outcome, not around features: migration done for them, volunteer training, and a member-launch kit to get members to adopt the app. Plus a clear offer name.
4. The founding-club offer goes to boards with a deadline, and at least three clubs sign before the platform is built further.
