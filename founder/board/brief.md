# Board brief: Greenside

Everything a board member needs; the founders' conversation is not available to you.
Facts below come from the founders and their own research (September 2026). Where a
number is the founders' estimate, it says so.

## The idea

- **What it is:** a white-label member app plus club management software ("clubbeheer")
  for Dutch golf clubs. Every club gets its own branded app (own name, logo and colours,
  own listing in the App Store and Google Play), all running on one shared platform and
  one codebase.
- **Who buys:** Dutch golf clubs with their own course. The decision is made by the club
  board; the software is used daily by the secretariat, the finance volunteer and the
  marshal (often volunteers). The members (golfers) use the app.
- **Who uses the app:** the club's members, from teenagers to retirees.

## What the product does today (a working pilot, frozen as "pilot-v1")

Member app:
- Log in with an e-mailed six-digit code (no passwords).
- Book tee times (9 or 18 holes), add club members or guests; the app counts how often a
  guest (introducé) played this year and charges the normal green fee after the limit.
- Extras at booking (buggy, Handicart rate for disabled golfers), lessons with the pro,
  seasonal storage, all charged to the member's account or paid with iDEAL.
- Club pass as a QR code for the driving-range ball machine; course status (open /
  restricted / closed) from the marshal.
- Competitions (sign up, results), digital scorecard with live Stableford points,
  handicap, hole sponsors on the scorecard.
- Invoices (membership fee, bar tab put on the member's name, purchases), invoice as PDF,
  IBAN for direct debit, digital membership card, member directory, introduce-a-friend,
  add a family member, upgrade membership, delete account.

Club management (web):
- "Mission control": what needs attention today, in plain language for volunteers.
- Members: import from the current system (CSV from E-Golf4U, Nexxchange, IntoGolf or
  Excel), invite all members to the app in one go, approve membership changes.
- Tee sheet with check-in, course status, competitions, news, products sold in the app,
  sponsors, finance (invoices, payments, bar tab on account), business statistics
  ("how often the licence has been earned back").
- Security work done: strict separation between clubs, login by code, audit.

Greenside HQ (internal): sales pipeline, create a club, per-club setup checklist and
branding.

## Price (founders' current pricing advice)

- € 249 / month: 9 holes, up to ±700 members.
- € 449 / month: 18 holes, ±700–1,100 members (the standard).
- € 599 / month: 27+ holes, from ±1,100 members.
- Founding clubs: € 399 / month, fixed for two years, for the first ten clubs.
- One-off migration: € 750 (9 holes) to € 1,500 (18+ holes).
- Three-month pilot, then a yearly contract; two months free when paying a year up front.
- No commission on what members buy in the app ("the money is the club's"); the founders
  say some platforms take up to 20% of the green fee.

## Market facts the founders collected (sources: NGF / golf.nl and others, 2026)

- 445,009 golfers in the Netherlands on 1 April 2026, +3.3% in a year.
- 263 golf clubs are NGF members; 275 courses.
- 53% of golfers are not a member of a club with a course.
- Average membership fee with unlimited play: € 1,337 a year (2026).
- Average green fee 18 holes: € 74.23 (2026).
- Membership fee is about 57% of a club's income; bar/restaurant 17%; green fees 14%;
  other (lessons, rentals, sponsoring, competitions, entry fees) 12%.
- Club costs are mostly salaries (48%) and course maintenance (23%).
- The national federation's app GOLF.NL is free for golfers, also books tee times, and
  has 325,300 active users. Clubs therefore don't like paying for plain booking.
- Golfers are rising, but members per club are falling: clubs need to keep members and
  earn more per member and per guest.

## Founders' own value estimate (an estimate, not measured)

For an average 18-hole club (±950 members, ±€ 1.8 million turnover a year) the founders
estimate the app is worth € 19,000–45,000 a year to the club: retained members, new
members via introduced guests and family, extra buggy and extras sales, guests' green
fees paid directly, weekend rounds for weekday members, sponsor slots, hours saved at the
desk and secretariat, and replacing other software. Note: the "pause instead of cancel"
retention feature from that estimate was removed from the pilot at the pilot club's
request; pausing and cancelling are now handled by the club's own administration.

## Competition the founders know of

- Club management / booking systems clubs already use: E-Golf4U, Nexxchange, IntoGolf.
- IntoGolf publishes six to eight club-branded apps under its own company in the
  App Store.
- GOLF.NL (the federation's free app) books tee times.
No market shares, prices or switching costs for these are known yet.

## Where the founders are

- Pre-revenue. Two founders; the software is built with an AI coding assistant.
- Pilot software finished and frozen ("pilot-v1"); a branded version for the first pilot
  club, Golfclub Zwolle (in its own colours and logo), is built but not live.
- Still to do before going live: production accounts (EU database, e-mail server for the
  login codes, hosting, Apple and Google developer accounts), legal documents (data
  processing agreement, privacy statement, terms), the pilot club's member data.
- Open product decisions: a separate login role for bar staff; per-club integration with
  the driving-range ball machine (some clubs use Xafax MyNetPay).
- No outside funding mentioned. No customer has paid yet.

## Unknown (do not guess; name it as an open question)

Market share of the incumbents, what clubs pay them today, switching costs and contract
terms, how many clubs will buy, the cost to win a club, churn, the founders' running
costs, and whether members will actually use a club's own app next to GOLF.NL.
