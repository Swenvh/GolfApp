# Product lens
Score: 6/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **The experience is real, not a deck.** There is a working pilot (pilot-v1) and a branded build for Golfclub Zwolle. The member flow (code login with no password, booking with guest-limit logic, the account, the QR pass, the live Stableford scorecard) starts from what a golfer actually does on a club day.
- **It is built for the real users.** "Mission control" in plain language for volunteers shows taste: it starts from the secretariat volunteer's morning and works back.
- **Details nobody sees were done.** Strict separation between clubs, an audit log, account deletion, the Handicart rate and the introducé counter. That is the craft the Product lens looks for.
- **A clean promise.** "No commission, the money is the club's" fits in one sentence.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No one thing.** The feature list covers booking, competitions, scorecard, handicap, invoicing, bar tab, lessons, storage, sponsors, the directory, finance and a CRM. GOLF.NL already books tee times for free, with 325,300 active users. If the one thing is "booking", it loses. If it is "everything", it does nothing brilliantly. The brief does not say which it is.
2. **Seams with systems it does not own.** Member data imported from E-Golf4U, Nexxchange or IntoGolf; handicap and results next to the federation; ball machines (Xafax MyNetPay is still undecided); direct debit; the bar tab without a bar-staff role. Each handover is where the member's experience breaks, and today the club's own administration owns pause and cancel, not the product.
3. **The member's first five minutes next to GOLF.NL.** Whether members will open a club app at all is listed as unknown. If adoption is low, the club's value case (€ 19,000–45,000, the founders' estimate) falls apart. One of its levers, "pause instead of cancel", has already been removed.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- In one sentence: what does a member do in your app that they cannot do in GOLF.NL, and how often?
- What does the first five minutes look like for a 70-year-old member who gets the invite e-mail, and for the volunteer on import day?
- What would you cut from pilot-v1 if you had to go live at Zwolle next month with half the features?
- Is this a replacement for E-Golf4U/Nexxchange/IntoGolf or a layer on top? Who owns the member record, and what happens when the two disagree?
- Who supports a club and its members when a login code does not arrive on a Saturday morning?
- What share of Zwolle members must use the app weekly for the club to call the pilot a success? (Measure this in the pilot; /founder-consumer can test intent beforehand.)

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Name the one thing in one sentence, and make the pilot's success metric about that thing (for example, weekly active members and in-app revenue the club would not otherwise have had), agreed with Zwolle before go-live.
2. Finish the seams before launch: production e-mail delivery for codes, legal documents, a decision on the bar-staff role and the ball machine, and a written answer to who owns the member record.
3. Go live at Zwolle and report real member adoption and club volunteer usage after the pilot before a second club is signed. Get the price and cost to win a club from /founder-cfo and how clubs compare to incumbents from /founder-competitors.
