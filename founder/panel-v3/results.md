## What would flip a no

- Golfclub Zwolle live for a few months with good member numbers, and their board telling me on the phone that tee times and invoicing run without problems.
- Our current vendor letting us down badly, plus a year of Golfclub Zwolle and a few more clubs running on it with results I can check and a board I can call.
- Golfclub Zwolle live for a full season, with their board telling me on the phone that members use it and the desk saved time, and a cost comparison showing we pay less in total than we do now.
- Our current system failing us or raising its price sharply, plus two or three clubs our size, beyond Zwolle, running on it for a full season without trouble.
- A call with the Zwolle board after a season live, plus a side-by-side sum showing that cancelling our current vendor makes Greenside cheaper or no more expensive in total.
- Golfclub Zwolle (or better, a club of our size) live for at least one full season with a good results page and a board that tells me on the phone it saved their staff work.
- Seeing Golfclub Zwolle live with real numbers (members active in the app, invoices collected without problems) and hearing their board say over the phone that the switch went smoothly.
- Seeing a club our size, not Zwolle, live for a full season with older members actually using it, and the total cost coming out at or below what we pay our current vendor.
- Seeing Golfclub Zwolle's results after a full season, with real numbers on older members actually using the app, and timing it for when our current contract comes up for renewal in a year or two.
- Zwolle's board telling me on the phone that it works, plus a cost comparison showing that once we cancel our current system the total doesn't go up.
- Seeing Zwolle's results page after a full season and hearing from their board that the bar tab, invoices and the SEPA file worked without extra evening work for the desk.
- Zwolle live for a full season with real numbers, ideally including more guest green fees or bar spend through the app, and their board telling me on the phone it was worth it.
