## What would flip a no

- Golfclub Zwolle live for a full season, with its board telling me on the phone that tee times, invoicing and the older members all went smoothly, plus a second club of our size on board.
- Written confirmation that it works with the NGF handicap and our direct debit, and a call with the Golfclub Zwolle board after they've been live for a full season. Then I'd look again at our next contract renewal.
- Zwolle live for a full season, their board telling me on the phone it went smoothly, and the bar tab linked to the card machine and cash register we already have.
- In about two years, a handful of comparable 18-hole clubs running it for a full season whose boards I can call, and proof the team has grown beyond two people.
- A side-by-side showing it replaces our current system so our total software bill stays the same or drops, plus a call with Golfclub Zwolle's board once they have been live for a while.
- Zwolle running live for a full season, and a board and a club manager there I can call who tell me it cut their workload, plus one more club of our size on it.
- Golfclub Zwolle live for a full season, with their board telling me on the phone that their older members actually use it, plus written confirmation of the link to the federation's handicap system.
- Seeing Golfclub Zwolle live for a season and hearing from their board that members actually use it and the bar tab and invoices run without problems, plus our current vendor failing to fix the phone booking complaints.
- Golfclub Zwolle live for a full season, with a board I can call who says older members actually use it and that Greenside did the migration work, not their volunteers. Then I'd bring it up when our current contract comes up for renewal.
- A call with the Golfclub Zwolle board after a few months live, showing members actually use it and it saves us real money or staff hours, so I can replace a cost instead of adding one.
- Golfclub Zwolle live for a season with their board telling me it's running smoothly, plus written confirmation that it links to the NGF handicap system and our SEPA direct debit, in time to put it on the spring members' meeting budget.
- A comparable 18-hole club that has been live for a full season, whose board tells me on the phone that it runs without problems and shows me more guest bookings or bar revenue through the app.
